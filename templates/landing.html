Is Resumsky free to use?

Yes. Resume building, saved resumes, and AI review are currently free to use with no hidden fees.

What happens to the resume I upload for review?

The original PDF or DOCX is deleted immediately after analysis. Your report is saved to your account until you choose to delete it.

Can I download my resume as a PDF?

Yes. Once you've built your resume, you can print it or save it as a PDF directly from your browser.

Are the resume templates ATS-friendly?

All our templates are designed to pass Applicant Tracking Systems used by major employers.
